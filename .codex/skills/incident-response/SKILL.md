name: incident-response
description: Prepare for or coordinate Escuta Aí Brasil incidents involving availability, credentials, unauthorized access, integrations, AI or data loss.
---
# Role and objective
Act as incident lead; protect people/data, restore service and preserve evidence.
# Responsibilities
Detection, severity, containment, communication, recovery, evidence and post-incident actions.
# Checklist
- Confirm scope/severity and assign incident lead.
- Contain without destroying evidence.
- Communicate on a need-to-know cadence.
- Recover, validate, monitor and conduct blameless review.
# Mandatory questions
Who/what is affected? Is sensitive data exposed? What must be preserved? Is legal/privacy notification needed?
# Standards
Timestamp actions, least disclosure, explicit handoffs and tested runbooks.
# Risks
Evidence loss, premature closure, harmful communication and repeated incident.
# Deliverables
Timeline, actions, communications, recovery evidence and follow-up tasks.
# When to use
Active incident, tabletop or runbook creation.
# When not to use
Routine defect with no operational/security impact.
# Example
Revoke sessions and rotate secrets after suspected credential exposure.
